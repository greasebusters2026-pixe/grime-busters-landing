Care between cleans

Small habits.
A cleaner workspace.

We source cleaning products locally. Ask us about the products proposed for your truck and their suitability for the surfaces being cleaned.

1

Clean before you sanitise

Remove food residue and grease first. Sanitising is a separate step, not a substitute for cleaning.

2

Let the label lead

Choose a product suitable for the surface. Follow its dilution, contact-time and rinsing instructions.

3

Keep products separate

Don't mix cleaning chemicals. Keep labelled products safely stored away from food.

Product information and cleaning guidance

Ask us for the product details for your clean. Check certification against the exact product, rather than relying on a general approval claim. Product certification does not certify the whole cleaning business.

General guidance: cleaning and sanitising surfaces; safe handling of cleaning products; SABS advice on checking approval claims.

Follow the product label and your food-safety procedures. Our cleaning service does not replace a health inspection or official food-premises certification.

Are the package prices the final price?

No. R800, R1,200 and R1,800 are starting prices. We confirm the cleaning scope and quote the call-out fee separately before you agree to the job.

Can I choose a cleaning date?

Yes, you can request a preferred date. Availability is confirmed by us. Your booking is secured after the quote and date are approved and the 35% deposit is verified.

How do I send photos of my truck?

Send them to 067 721 4589 on WhatsApp, together with your name and truck location. Photos are sent in WhatsApp, not uploaded through this website.

How do payments work?

We use iKhokha. After your quote and date are agreed, ask us for the payment link for your 35% deposit. The remaining 65% is due on the service day. This website does not collect card details.

Can I cancel or move my booking?

Please give at least 48 hours' notice so we can make alternative arrangements. Replacement dates depend on availability. Contact us as soon as possible if your plans change. Ask for the full cancellation terms before paying.

Do you clean outside Cape Town?

Cape Town is our current focus. Expansion is a future goal, not a promise of nationwide availability. Contact us with your location so we can discuss your request.
