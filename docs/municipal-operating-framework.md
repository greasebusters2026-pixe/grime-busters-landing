# Grease Busters: municipal delivery framework

Status: implementation framework, not a legal compliance certificate.
Reviewed: 30 October 2026.

## Evidence and agreement status

The owner reports municipal approval and requests use of the municipal logo. The available business dossier contains a municipal concept proposal, not the signed agreement. Record the actual agreement reference, parties, authorised signatories, scope, sites, start/end dates, required reporting, acceptance criteria, insurance limits, payment terms and branding conditions in a PRIVATE contract register. Do not place that agreement, personal identity evidence, customer records or signatures in this public repository.

Public website wording describes an independent provider and does not infer supplier accreditation, delegated inspection powers, an exclusive partnership, a contract award value or authority to issue Certificates of Acceptability. Replace or extend wording only within the actual approval scope.

## Job gates

1. Intake: identify premises, requested work, site contact, relevant inspection findings and approved scope. Keep food truck packages distinct from commercial or municipal quotes.
2. Authorisation: confirm the written instruction, scope, pricing, lawful procurement route where relevant and site permissions. A lead or proposal is not authority to start municipal work. The private 35% deposit policy does not set institutional terms.
3. Preparation: check safe access, equipment isolation, crew competence, chemical labels/SDS, PPE and containment. Escalate unsafe access, unsupported specialist work or a missing disposal route. No confined-space entry under the ordinary cleaning scope.
4. Delivery: use the equipment manufacturer's method and agreed task procedures. Record visible defects and completed work. Do not describe a visual check as structural certification. Do not flush recovered grease into sewers or stormwater. Do not assume a biodegradable chemical is safe for discharge or compatible with a grease separator.
5. Waste: keep trap contents distinct from separately collected used fryer oil. Record actual description, measured/estimated quantity and units, transporter, manifest and destination. Obtain written acceptance for the material before transport; retain the receiving receipt after transfer.
6. Close-out: acknowledge completed work, list remaining actions with owners and dates, and link supporting evidence. If a receipt is outstanding, use 'awaiting disposal evidence'; do not close it as verified recycling.
7. Review: schedule according to actual trap type, use, manufacturer's instructions and applicable permit conditions. Do not treat 25% or 4–8 weeks as a universal Cape Town legal rule.

## Records and environmental claims

Use `service-record.html` as a blank printable template. Store completed records in a controlled private system, never GitHub. Keep waste-transfer certificates at least two years according to the City's published guidance, subject to longer applicable requirements. The website has no authentication or client document portal.

Track service visits, unresolved corrective actions, separately collected quantities and receiving evidence. Do not count an entire tanker load as recovered oil, convert litres to kilograms without a justified basis, or claim avoided emissions / zero landfill without supporting methodology and records. Youth employment and customer growth targets on the impact page are plans, not achieved results.
