The service file

From instruction
to close-out.

  1. 1

    Instruction

    Written scope, site and asset IDs, customer contact, permission for photos, commercial terms and any contract or order reference.

  2. 2

    Preparation

    Task risk assessment, equipment checks, chemical safety information, crew suitability and spill controls. Resolve missing requirements before starting.

  3. 3

    Service

    Visit date, technician, work completed, visible defects, before-and-after evidence and customer acknowledgement.

  4. 4

    Follow-up

    Action owner, due date, re-check result and receiving-facility evidence where waste moved. Open items remain recorded until resolved.

Two distinct waste streams

Track the material.
Confirm its destination.

Grease-trap contents and used fryer oil need separate records and suitable receiving routes.

Specialist extraction and recovery remain subject to equipment, operator and partner verification before delivery.

What belongs in the record?

Grease-trap contents: grease, settled solids and wastewater. Record the actual waste description and quantity; confirm facility acceptance before collection.

Used cooking oil: separately collected fryer oil. Keep it separate from trap sludge and record the collector and accepted recovery destination.

Close-out: link the manifest to the receiving receipt. Mark a missing receipt as “awaiting disposal evidence”. Claim recycling only when downstream records support it.

Municipal & institutional projects

Agree the project
before mobilising.

Each project needs its own authorised scope, reporting requirements, acceptance criteria and payment terms.

BEFORE AWARD / START

Supplier evidence

Provide the applicable registration, tax, ownership, insurance, workforce and safety documents through the agreed procurement channel. Confirm validity and scope for the specific project.

BEFORE FIELDWORK

Authorised instruction

Confirm the responsible official, contract or order reference, approved sites, work limits and reporting timetable in writing. Private food truck deposit terms do not set municipal contract terms.

BEFORE CLOSE-OUT

Accepted deliverables

Submit the agreed service records, open-action list and applicable waste evidence. Resolve queries and record acceptance against the project’s actual requirements.
