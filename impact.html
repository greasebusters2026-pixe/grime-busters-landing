Impact & partnerships / Cape Town

Clean kitchens are the start.
A circular future is the goal.

Grease Busters is building from food truck cleaning towards a model that connects service records, responsible grease management and local employment.

This is our development roadmap. Waste recovery, specialist extraction and the digital client portal are planned capabilities, not services currently included in our cleaning packages.

The problem we want to solve

Grease needs
a responsible route.

Commercial kitchens need practical support to manage grease, maintain cleaning records and understand what happens to collected material.

PREVENT

Keep grease under control

Build regular cleaning relationships and help customers identify where specialist grease management is needed.

DOCUMENT

Make work traceable

Develop a digital logbook linking each visit to its scope, photos, service record and any applicable waste documents.

RECOVER

Build responsible partnerships

Explore used cooking oil collection through appropriate recovery partners, including potential biodiesel outlets. Keep oil recovery separate from grease-trap sludge treatment.

A phased operating model

Prove each step.
Then scale.

  1. 1

    Current foundation

    Food truck cleaning and degreasing in Cape Town, with scope and pricing agreed before booking.

  2. 2

    Prepare specialist work

    Obtain equipment quotes, assess vacuum extraction and pressure-cleaning requirements, and verify safety controls and waste partners.

  3. 3

    Pilot traceability

    Test service records, collection records and destination evidence before launching secure client access.

  4. 4

    Measure and grow

    Review customer retention, operating costs, waste evidence and paid employment before expanding.

ENVIRONMENTAL BASELINE

Equipment & enabling support

Fund the capacity
to deliver.

We are seeking conversations with equipment suppliers, responsible waste operators, training partners and funders.

Our quote schedule will assess extraction pumps and compatible tanks, pressure-cleaning equipment, hoses and fittings, spill-containment equipment, PPE and training. Final specifications and costs depend on the work scope and supplier advice.

Evidence before expansion

Build a reviewable partner pack.

  • Current business registration and procurement documents
  • Supplier specifications and written equipment quotations
  • Waste partner permissions and destination agreements
  • Safety procedures, training and insurance evidence
  • Costed hiring plan and cash-flow forecast

Funding limits, eligibility and permitted purchases will be checked against current programme rules before any application.
